# Acme Robotics Q2 preview: lidar shipments expected to double

**Date:** 2026-07-02 · **Source:** Example Research · **Company:** [[acme-robotics|Acme Robotics]]

## Report

Example Research expects Acme Robotics to ship 120,000 lidar units in Q2, double a year earlier,
helped by two new car-maker customers. It keeps a Buy rating and a target price of USD 18.0.
Gross margin is expected at 14%, up from 9%, as the new sensor costs less to build.
