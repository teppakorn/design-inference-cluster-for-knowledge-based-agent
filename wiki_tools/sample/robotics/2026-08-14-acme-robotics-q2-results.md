# Acme Robotics Q2 results beat on margin, target price cut to USD 16.5

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Source:** Sample Securities · **Company:** [[acme-robotics|Acme Robotics]]

## Report

Q2 lidar shipments were 104,000 units, below the 120,000 expected, but gross margin reached 15.2%.
Sample Securities cuts its target price from USD 19.0 to USD 16.5 on slower car-maker orders in H2,
and keeps a Hold rating. Robot-vacuum sensors grew to 30% of revenue.
